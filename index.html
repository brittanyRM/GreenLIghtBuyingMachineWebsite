I build I buy

You're bidding on the same houses as everyone else.

Every flipper in the Valley is competing for the same MLS inventory and selling to the same retail buyer pool. Margins thin out, days on market stretch, and the exit depends on what a family with a mortgage will pay that month.

Co-living changes what a house is worth, because it changes who buys it. A property converted to shared housing sells to an investor pricing off room-level income, not comps down the street.

The catch is that the learning curve is unforgiving. Room count, egress, parking, bathroom ratios, city occupancy rules, the finish standard operators expect — get one wrong and a good deal becomes an expensive education.

This program exists so you don't pay for that education with your own capital. You bring the crew and the execution. We bring the deal, the standard, and the buyer.
