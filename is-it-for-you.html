Before you apply

Is it for you?

We're looking for operators with 10 to 15 flips behind them and a crew already working. Being honest about that up front saves everyone a hard conversation three weeks in.

A fit if you

  • Have 10 to 15 completed flips behind you
  • Already run your own crew — not trades you'd have to go find
  • Buy and finance your own deals: first, second, hard money, however you normally stack it
  • Are ready to own the entire buildout and every decision in it
  • Want a different exit, not a different hobby
  • Can work an Arizona property — that's where our deal flow and buyers are today
  • Can cover the onboarding fee and monthly subscription on top of the deal itself
  • Carry general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence

Not a fit if you

  • Are early in your flipping career
  • Would need to assemble a crew first
  • Are looking for passive income
  • Want to assign the contract instead of building
  • Need someone else to manage the build
  • Are shopping for a guarantee

What members agree to

These aren't aspirations — they're in the agreement you'd sign.

  • Submit accurate, complete documentation for certification
  • Attend coaching — weekly the first month, every other week after
  • Provide video walkthroughs of the property
  • Maintain compliance records
  • Carry general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence
  • Conduct all negotiations independently
  • Comply with all city, county, state and federal building codes, zoning, safety and occupancy law

You also take sole responsibility for permits, code compliance, contractor selection, legally required inspections, and habitability — and for your own due diligence on acquisition, financing, renovation feasibility, zoning, co-living legality, occupancy limits and resale value. We coach you through all of it. We don't verify any of it for you.

Not in Arizona?

Arizona is where we operate today. The processes, the lender relationship, the registered buyers, and twenty-six years of knowing which streets work — all of it is here, and that's most of what you'd be paying for.

We're building toward other markets. We'd rather do that properly than plant a flag somewhere we can't yet source a deal or bring you a buyer.

So apply anyway. Tell us where you operate, and we'll put you on the list for your market. Where we go next is going to be decided by where the qualified operators are — which means the applications we get.

Straight answers

Can I bring my own deal?

Yes, and we'll evaluate it with you first. Not every single-storey house converts — layout, egress, bathroom placement, parking and occupancy rules all decide it, and that varies house by house and city by city. We'll walk through what to look at. Confirming legal permissibility for a given property is your responsibility, not something we verify on your behalf.

What if the renovation goes sideways?

It's your project and your capital, so the risk is real. What the program changes is that you have people who have done this before looking at the same problem, and checkpoints designed to catch trouble before it compounds.

Is the buyer guaranteed?

No, and our agreement says so in plain terms. The Marketplace Module gives your certified property listing visibility to registered buyers. We don't match buyers with sellers, negotiate, draft contracts, hold escrow, or represent either party, and we don't guarantee that a property sells, that any buyer is interested, or the timing or price of a sale. Every transaction happens directly between you and the buyer. What we do bring is an audience already looking for co-living assets and a certification that tells them what they're looking at.

Does certification mean the house is approved or up to code?

No. Certification confirms that the documentation you submitted meets our standards. It is not government approval, a code compliance certificate, zoning confirmation, or a safety guarantee, and we don't perform physical inspections. Permits, inspections, code compliance, occupancy rules and habitability all stay with you.

How long does coaching last?

For as long as your membership is active. Coaching runs weekly through your first month and every other week after that, and the agreement is month to month. If coaching is meant to continue after a subscription ends, that needs to be written into the MSA — right now the two say different things.

When does the next cohort start?

There isn't a start date to wait for. Enrollment is open and you begin when you're accepted and onboarded.

Do I have to be in Arizona?

You don't have to live here, but today the property does. Cohorts are Arizona-based because that's where our deal flow, our lender and our buyers are. We're working toward other markets, and applications from out-of-state operators are how we decide which one comes next — so apply and tell us where you are.

What if the renovation runs long?

Same as any flip — it's your holding cost and your schedule. What changes is that your buyer is being lined up while you work rather than after you list, so the back end of the timeline isn't sitting on a market you can't control.
